BIO

Born from deep personal adversity and unrelenting survival, S0RD is a darkwave/post-punk project based out of Oklahoma City. Following years of severe trial—from surviving homelessness and joblessness to overcoming deep personal betrayal—S0RD turned entirely to music as a vital lifeline.

At the core of the S0RD sound are driving electric basslines anchored by striking guitar leads—seamlessly shifting between syncopated, bite-heavy crunch and expansive, dreamy atmospheres. Sparse, selective melodic accents (like glassy bell tones and dark organ patches) frame the arrangement, but the heavy rhythmic push and dual guitar/bass dynamics carry the weight. Taking full control of composition, production, and performance, S0RD delivers pure artistic independence, turning real-life grit into raw post-punk urgency.
